# Code of Conduct

Better San Fernando is an independent civic information project. Everyone who participates in its issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Take care with public-service information: be accurate, cite sources, and flag uncertainty.
- Protect other people's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Knowingly submitting false or misleading civic information.
- Using project spaces for spam, intimidation, or political campaigning.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions, and to people representing the project publicly.

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports and may remove content or restrict participation when needed. They will handle reports with care and share details only with people who need them to respond.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
